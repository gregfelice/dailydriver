


WHAT NIGHTPANEL IS
- a saab-inspired dark mode for your desktop that enhances focus designed to work flawlessly on debian and gnome

WHAT NIGHTPANNEL DOES
- gives you slick desktop management capabilites for use day or night
  - slimfox - a super-minimal firefox customization, oriented towards keyboard users
  - tiling and keymap management - inspired by hyprland keymaps and tiling behaviors, oriented towards keyboard users
  - minimal tools and keymaps you use the most - terminals, browsers, music players, screenshots
- gives you a "NIGHT PANEL" button on your task bar, when toggled, you "go dark" - 
  with all screen colors and elements transformed into their nighttime, low distraction equivalent
  when you toggle it off, you return to your favorite regular schemes

WHAT NIGHTPANEL CONFIGURES
- colors
- text capitalization rules
- fonts
- icons (where possible)
